ScratchPro Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026

ScratchPro is a personal open source project based on TurboWarp. It has no accounts and does not collect personal information. Projects you make are kept on your own device unless you choose to save or share them somewhere else.

Hosting

ScratchPro is hosted on Netlify, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, when you visit. See Netlify's privacy policy.

Loading projects and the sprite library

When you open a Scratch project by its ID or link, or use the built-in sprite, sound and backdrop library, your browser downloads them from Scratch's servers (scratch.mit.edu). Project details and thumbnails are fetched through TurboWarp's project service (trampoline.turbowarp.org). These services receive your IP address as part of the request.

Settings

Editor settings and addon choices are stored in your browser's local storage and never leave your device.

Source code

The source code is available on GitHub. Questions can be raised there as an issue.
